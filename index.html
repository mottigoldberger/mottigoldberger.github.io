Motti Goldberger

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Operations at the Yale School of Management, advised by Nils Rudi. Before Yale, I received a B.S. in Mathematics from the University of Maryland.

My research focuses on sequential learning and adaptive data collection, with an emphasis on developing algorithms for efficiently collecting information for ranking and selection. Much of my work is motivated by settings involving human evaluators, who often provide feedback in the form of pairwise comparisons or ratings. Applications of my research include LLM evaluation, hiring and admissions, clinical trials, simulation optimization, and tournament design.
